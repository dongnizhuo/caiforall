K-8 Research Curriculum

A Scratch-based curriculum for computational thinking and AI.

We design and study weekly, culturally relevant K-8 curricula that help every student develop computational thinking, coding fluency, and AI literacy — alongside the teacher resources and professional development to make it work in real classrooms.

Curriculum

Two grade-banded curricula, one shared mission.

From elementary creators to middle-school AI builders, our materials grow with students — and the research grows with them.

Grade 3–5

Create · Design · Impact

Three years of a weekly, interdisciplinary, culturally relevant elementary curriculum. Students build computational thinking through engaging activities and projects.

  • Year 1 & 2: computational thinking through relevant learning activities
  • Year 3: coding integrated with community-based environmental and data literacy
  • Designed for absolute beginners — no prior coding experience required
Explore Grade 3–5
Grade 6–8

STEM + AI

An integrated, semester-long coding curriculum using CreatiCode — a Scratch-based platform for AI chatbots, 3D modeling, and AI-assisted creation.

  • Five units exploring generative AI tools and applications
  • Build voice and vision AI apps in a blocks-based environment
  • Advanced topics: neural networks, self-driving cars, and more
Explore Grade 6–8
Designed for the classroom

Built around the people who use it.

Every resource is shaped by years of co-design with teachers and the students they serve.

For Students

Learners, K–8
  • Structured inquiry involves students in creative coding while providing the step-by-step guidance absolute beginners need to succeed.
  • Language support helps English learners and students with reading challenges develop literacy and computing skills simultaneously.
  • STEM identity grows as children learn about diverse computer scientists and code stories about their own lives and communities.

For Teachers

Educators & Professional Development
  • Instructional resources — detailed lesson plans, notes, handouts, and slides — make teaching the material easy.
  • Professional development is designed so that no prior coding experience is necessary to start teaching the Grade 3–5 curriculum.
  • Assessment tools include a computational thinking test, identity survey, observation protocol, and student reflection forms and rubrics.
